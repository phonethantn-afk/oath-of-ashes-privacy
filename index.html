Oath of Ashes — Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026

Oath of Ashes is a single-player game for Android, made by Lionnes. This page explains what the game does with information about you. In short: the game itself collects nothing; the optional ads, provided by Google AdMob, do.

What the game collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no tracking of our own. We never ask for or receive your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

What stays on your device

Your progress (trials won, gold, equipment, your knight’s name and look) and your settings are saved only on your device, in the game’s own storage. They are never sent to us or anyone else, and they are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded ads only, and only when you choose to watch one (to double the gold from a won trial, or to rise again once after falling). There are no banners or pop-up ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which uses its own software (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) inside the game. To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, it automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • your device’s IP address, which may be used to estimate its general location;
  • interactions such as app launches, taps and ad views;
  • diagnostic information about the app’s and the ads’ performance;
  • device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.

This data is sent encrypted. Google’s use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google’s Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone’s settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Google › Ads). If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Google’s consent form asks for your choice before any ad is requested, and you can change it later in the game under Settings › About › Ad privacy options. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens.

The game needs internet access only for these ads. Everything else works offline, and if you never watch an ad, no ad is requested.

Permissions

Vibration, for haptic feedback (you can switch haptics off in Settings), and internet access, for the optional ads.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Google Play

The game is distributed through Google Play; your Google Play account and downloads are covered by Google’s Privacy Policy.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date.
